# Skill: interview-prep

Prepare the candidate for an interview for a job in `job_tracker`.

## 1. Gather
- The `job_tracker` row and the full posting; anything the candidate sent with the application (stay consistent with it).
- `Context/About_Me.md`, the CV(s), `Context/Target.md`.
- Ask: stage and format (HR screen, hiring manager, case, technical, panel), language, interviewers' names, date and time.

## 2. Research (cite sources)
Company products, technology, customers, business model, stage, funding, competitors, recent news, culture; interviewers' public professional background if the candidate shares names (professional information only); how the role fits the company's priorities.

## 3. Content
- One-page company brief.
- 12–15 likely questions: motivation ("why us", "why this role", "why this city"), role-specific, behavioural, a short case or technical question if typical for the role.
- The candidate's best examples in STAR format, taken only from their real experience (About_Me.md), mapped to the questions.
- Tricky points and how to handle them (risks from `job_tracker`: experience level, career change, certifications in progress, language, relocation and start date, visa).
- 5–8 questions for the candidate to ask.
- A 60-second "tell me about yourself" for this role, in the interview language.

## 4. Output
A document the candidate can keep and edit (in the folder `Interviews/`, named `<Company>_<role>_interview_prep`). Update "Next step and date" in `job_tracker` with the interview date (only if the workbook isn't open). Offer a mock interview in the chat, with feedback.
